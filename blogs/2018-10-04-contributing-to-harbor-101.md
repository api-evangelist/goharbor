We’re elated at the increased community involvement since the donation of Harbor to the Cloud Native Computing Foundation. There’s lots of interest around the project, including a steady buzz of activity in our Slack channels and on GitHub. This blog post is for those interested in contributing to a CNCF project that may not have much experience with the various steps involved.
