TL;DR; Artifact signing and signature verification are critical security capabilities that allow you to verify the integrity of an artifact. Harbor supports content trust through integrations with Notary and Cosign. Harbor v2.5 integrates support for Cosign, a OCI artifact signing and verification solution that is part of the Sigstore project.
