We’ve been hard at work on our new Harbor site, and we’re thrilled to finally open it up to the world! For those unfamiliar with Harbor, it is an open source cloud native registry with a focus on storing, scanning and securing content. Born out of a need for such a project at VMware, the registry’s roadmap and feature velocity is influenced by our community of users and developers.
