Harbor Moves to the CNCF Incubator We are thrilled to announce the promotion of Harbor from the Cloud Native Sandbox to Incubating status as a CNCF project. Harbor is an open source cloud native registry project that stores, signs, and scans content. This milestone reflects Harbor’s significant achievements in the areas of community involvement, governance, feature enhancements and adoption.
